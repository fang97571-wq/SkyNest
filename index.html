About SkyNest

Modern co-living built around people, flexibility and community.

Who we are

SkyNest Co-Living Spaces is a modern co-living company based in Singapore. We provide flexible, well-designed accommodation for young professionals, digital nomads and international students.

We believe a home should be more than a room. That is why every SkyNest residence combines private space with shared areas to work, relax and meet people.

Our values

Our mission

To make living in Singapore simple, affordable and social, with no long contracts and no hidden surprises.

Our vision

A city where newcomers feel at home from day one and where communities grow naturally through shared spaces.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about living at SkyNest.

How long is the minimum stay?

Standard Stay starts at 3 months. Short-Term Stay is available from 7 nights.

What is included in the price?

Utilities, Wi-Fi, access to all shared facilities and regular cleaning of common areas.

Are the rooms furnished?

Yes. Every room comes with a bed, desk, storage and bedding.

Can I have visitors?

Yes, visitors are welcome in shared areas during visiting hours.

Is the building accessible?

Our buildings include lifts and step-free access. Contact us for details about accessible rooms.

How do I book?

Choose a package, then send us an enquiry through the Contact page. We reply within one working day.
